# Surviving Mars: Detailed Progression & Item Catalog Mind Map (IDs Removed)

* **Central Idea: Surviving Mars - Detailed Progression & Item Catalog**

    * **Main Branch 1: Game Progression Stages**
        * **Sub-branch: Initial Landing & Basic Survival (TL0/TL1 Start)**
            * Arrival & Imperatives (Survival, Funding)
            * Initial Tools (Construct, Extractor, Scanner, Place ENVAS, GrapplingHookTool)
            * Resource Gathering (Iron, Copper, Ice) -> Water Production
            * Basic Shelter (Walls, Old Door) & Storage (Small Box, Crate)
            * Early Economy (Recovery Contracts, Credit Wallet)
        * **Sub-branch: Shelter & Foundational Tech (TL1)**
            * Power Grid Setup (Habitat Power Connector, Wind Turbine, Power Line)
            * Core Crafting (Workbench -> Iron rod, Concrete)
            * Habitat Construction (Habitat Module, Airlock, Connector)
            * Initial Investment (Blueprint Terminal -> Purchase Blueprints)
        * **Sub-branch: Genesis of Industry (TL1 End / TL2 Start)**
            * Workbench Expansion (Steel beam, Copper ingot/spool, Aluminum bar, Polymer -> Plastic)
            * Essential Devices (Liquid Processor, Food Processor -> Tea)
            * Basic Needs (Oxygen cannister, Bed)
            * Tier 1 Exports (Raw Materials via Makeshift Launch/Rocket) -> Funding TL2
        * **Sub-branch: Dawn of Advancement (TL2)**
            * Key Stations (Furnace, Machining Stn, Furniture Crafter, Cannister Maker, Planter Box, Fuel Stn/Storage, Upgrade Stn, Research Stn)
            * New Capabilities (Agriculture, Basic Food Recipes, Suit Upgrades T1)
            * Economic Growth (Tier 2 Exports - Refined Materials, Food) -> Funding TL3
        * **Sub-branch: Expanding Horizons (TL3)**
            * Advanced Stations (Chem Stn, Tech Stn, Recycler)
            * New Materials & Infrastructure (Alloys, Advanced Materials, Solar Panels, Rover Platform, Antenna)
            * Enhanced Player (Suit Upgrades T2-3, Cold Protection)
            * Economic Engine Matures (Tier 3 Exports - Components, Chemicals; Rocket Development) -> Funding TL4
        * **Sub-branch: Towards Martian Mastery (TL4 & TL5)**
            * TL4 Infrastructure (Computer Processor, RV Platform, Portal, Large Storage)
            * TL4 Player Capabilities (Suit Upgrades T4, Heat Protection)
            * TL5 Apex Technology (Nuclear Reactor, RTG, Radiation Protection, Suit Upgrades T5+)
            * Mature Rocket Economy (T4/T5 Exports - High-Value Goods) -> Funding End Game/Expansion
        * **Sub-branch: Colony Expansion & Sustenance (Late Game)**
            * Habitat Expansion (Vertical/Horizontal)
            * Advanced Logistics & Optimization
            * Quality of Life Improvements (Furniture, Decor)
            * Sustained High-Tier Operations & Exports
